HP change for Cores

Dota 2 · patch 7.42

Patch 7.42 (18 Nov 2026) gives heroes more base HP but less HP per point of Strength. Here is what that means for real heroes with the items they usually have.

Before (7.41f)HP = 120 + 22 × STR
After (7.42)HP = 200 + 20 × STR
Break-even40 STR: below gains HP, above loses

Takeaway: the patch favors Agility carries. Everyone starts with more HP. From level 8, Strength heroes lose HP (-2.3%) while Agility carries break even (+0.3%). At level 25: Strength -5.6%, Agility -5.0%. Strength heroes still have more HP, but their lead shrinks by 35 HP at level 8 and 52 HP at level 25.

How it was calculated
  • Groups: Agility carries are Agility heroes that Valve tags as "Carry": 31 heroes, 32 before the patch because Meepo became Universal in 7.42. Strength heroes are all 36 Strength heroes.
  • Strength: base STR + STR gain per level. The 7.41f column uses the old values for heroes whose STR changed in 7.42. Talents, facets and innate abilities are not counted.
  • Items: STRATZ data from the week of 12 Nov 2026 (patch 7.41f), all ranks and roles, 18,434 to 715,751 games per hero. Each item counts by the share of games in which it is owned. Components used up in a recipe are removed, and items sold later are not tracked. Both columns use these builds; Harpoon, Heaven's Halberd, Ethereal Blade and Linken's Sphere use their new 7.42 stats in the 7.42 column.
  • Level timing: each hero's own average XP curve gives the minute it reaches a level: level 4 at minute 4–7, level 8 at 10–16, level 15 at 22–31 and level 25 at 39–62. The headers show the typical minute. The XP data ends at minute 35, so level 25 is extrapolated.
  • Boots: STRATZ only gives an average purchase time for boots, so ownership is spread around that time. Brown Boots are assumed about 3 minutes before Treads, Phase and other upgrades.
  • Other choices: Armlet is counted switched off. Power Treads add +10 STR only for Strength heroes. Level 25 values are on the low side, because games that ended earlier are counted too.

Sources: Valve 7.42 patch notes · Valve hero data · STRATZ (item purchases, item stats and XP, via its API)
